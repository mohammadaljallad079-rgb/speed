### Defects fixed in this execution (each with a regression test)

1. `bookings/page.tsx` used `<DollarSign />` without importing it (TS2304) — the page failed to typecheck. Imported.
2. `bookings/page.tsx` and `rentals/[id]/page.tsx` approved a booking by inserting an
   invoice **in the browser** with a `INV-<row count>` number — collision-prone, no
   tenant, no journal. Both now issue through `POST /api/invoices`.
3. `rentals/[id]/page.tsx` numbered a receipt `REC-<row count>` — collision-prone.
   Now `nextDocumentNumber(..., 'receipt', 'REC')`.
4. `payments/new/page.tsx` saved a customer receipt with **no journal entry**, and
   numbered it `REC-<row count>`. Now posts `Dr Cash/Bank, Cr AR` and uses the RPC.
5. `POST /api/invoices` never wrote `invoices.amount` (the pre-VAT subtotal the print
   page, the detail form and the Excel export read), so a server-issued invoice
   printed a `0.000` line item. The row now carries `amount`; the print page also
   falls back to `total − VAT + discount` for legacy rows.
6. `invoices/[id]/page.tsx` allowed editing an invoice that already had payments or
   returns; now refused while dependents exist.
